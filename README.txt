Gift Vault V5.1

Private, local gift-card vault for iPhone.
- Encrypted local vault
- Expiry tracking inside the app
- Search, categories, favorites and redemption history
- Encrypted backup/restore
- PWA/offline shell

No push notifications or server-side reminder service is included. Expiry status is calculated locally when the app is opened.
